Privacy Policy

Dreadline: Zombie Apocalypse (iOS) · Nightfall Studio · Effective November 13, 2026

In short: Dreadline does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information, and it does not send gameplay data to us or to any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example unlocked characters, coins, language, sound and control layout) are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Dreadline is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing screenshots

If you tap the in-game share button, the game creates a screenshot and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose a destination yourself, and whatever you share goes directly to the app or person you picked, not to us.

Third-party services

The game does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social login SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that involves data (for example Game Center leaderboards provided by Apple), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the game's App Store page.

隐私政策（中文）

简而言之：《Dreadline: Zombie Apocalypse》不收集、不上传、不出售也不共享任何个人数据。游戏没有账号、没有广告、没有统计分析，也不追踪用户。游戏进度只保存在你的设备上。

  • 收集的信息：无。游戏不会获取你的姓名、邮箱、位置、通讯录、照片、设备标识符等任何个人信息。
  • 本地数据：进度和设置仅保存在本机，删除应用即一并删除。
  • 购买：本游戏为付费应用，付款全部由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接触你的支付信息。
  • 分享截图：只有在你主动点击分享按钮并选择目标时，截图才会通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的应用或联系人，不会发送给我们。
  • 第三方服务：不包含广告、统计、崩溃上报或社交登录 SDK。
  • 变更：如果之后的版本加入涉及数据的功能（例如 Apple 提供的 Game Center 排行榜），我们会在发布前更新本页面和 App Store 隐私信息。
