Privacy Policy

Dreadline: Zombie Apocalypse (iOS) · Nightfall Studio · Updated November 14, 2026

In short: Dreadline has no accounts and we do not collect personal data ourselves. Your progress stays on your device. The game offers optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob (for example, watch an ad to revive or to double a run's coins); when you choose to watch one, Google may process device and advertising data as described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example unlocked characters, coins, language, sound and control layout) are saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This data never leaves your device through the game. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Dreadline is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Sharing screenshots

If you tap the in-game share button, the game creates a screenshot and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose a destination yourself, and whatever you share goes directly to the app or person you picked, not to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show optional rewarded ads. Watching an ad is always your choice. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as device identifiers (for example the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Other third-party services

Apart from AdMob, the game does not include analytics, crash reporting or social login SDKs.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds another feature that involves data (for example Game Center leaderboards provided by Apple), we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the game's App Store page.

隐私政策（中文）

  • 本地数据：进度和设置仅保存在本机，删除应用即一并删除。
  • 购买：本游戏为付费应用，付款全部由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接触你的支付信息。
  • 分享截图：只有在你主动点击分享按钮并选择目标时，截图才会通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的应用或联系人，不会发送给我们。
  • 广告（Google AdMob）：游戏内置 Google 移动广告 SDK，用于展示可选的激励广告，看不看由你决定。为投放和统计广告、防止作弊，Google 可能收集设备标识符（广告标识符仅在你允许跟踪时使用）、IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置、设备和应用信息以及广告互动数据。在法律要求的地区（如欧洲经济区和英国），展示个性化广告前会通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意。详见 Google 隐私政策：policies.google.com/privacy。
  • 其他第三方服务：除 AdMob 外，不包含统计、崩溃上报或社交登录 SDK。
  • 变更：如果之后的版本加入涉及数据的功能（例如 Apple 提供的 Game Center 排行榜），我们会在发布前更新本页面和 App Store 隐私信息。
